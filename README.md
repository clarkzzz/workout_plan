# Foundations — Beginner Strength Plan

A structured, beginner-friendly 6-week strength training program designed to build a strong movement baseline, improve functional mobility, and develop sustainable workout habits without burnout or injury.

---

## Program Objectives

1. **Master the Fundamental Movement Patterns**  
   Focus on form and motor control across the core human movement patterns: **squat**, **hip hinge**, **horizontal pull**, **horizontal push**, **vertical press**, **unilateral leg work**, and **multi-plane core stability**.

2. **Form-First Progression**  
   Prioritize movement quality, range of motion, and joint health before increasing resistance. Clean mechanics produce faster, injury-free long-term strength gains.

3. **Accessible & Minimal Equipment**  
   Designed to be completed at home or in any gym with minimal gear:
   - A pair of light-to-moderate **dumbbells**
   - One light-to-moderate **kettlebell**
   - An **exercise mat**

4. **Balanced Full-Body Stimulus & Recovery**  
   Three non-consecutive training days per week allow adequate recovery for muscles, tendons, and the nervous system—crucial for beginner adaptation.

---

## Weekly Training Schedule

Train 3 days per week with at least one rest or active-recovery day between sessions:

| Day | Focus | Description | Primary Equipment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | **Lower Emphasis** *(Full Body A)* | Squat and hinge led session with bilateral rowing and overhead pressing. | Kettlebell, Dumbbells, Mat |
| **Day 2** | *Rest / Active Recovery* | Walking, light mobility, or complete rest. | — |
| **Day 3** | **Upper Emphasis** *(Full Body B)* | Pressing and pulling focus paired with lunges and shoulder/core mobility. | Dumbbells, Kettlebell, Mat |
| **Day 4** | *Rest / Active Recovery* | Walking, light mobility, or complete rest. | — |
| **Day 5** | **Power & Core** *(Full Body C)* | Dual-dumbbell strength, explosive kettlebell hip power, and a 360° core finisher. | Dumbbells, Kettlebell, Mat |
| **Days 6–7** | *Rest / Weekend Recovery* | Recharge for the next week. | — |

## Workout Breakdown

### Session Structure
Every workout follows a three-phase structure:
1. **Dynamic Warm-Up (5–8 min)**: Prepares joints and nervous system (marching in place, bodyweight squats, arm circles, cat-cows, hip circles).
2. **Main Strength Circuit (6 exercises)**: Performed with 60–90 seconds of rest between sets.
3. **Cooldown (3–5 min)**: Easy stretching targeting tight areas (hips, hamstrings, chest, shoulders).

---

### Day 1: Full Body Strength A (Lower Emphasis)
Lower-body led session establishing the squat and hinge mechanics while balancing upper-body push and pull.

1. **Kettlebell Goblet Squat** (`3 × 10`) — Core squat foundation; anterior load reinforces an upright torso.
2. **Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift** (`3 × 10`) — Posterior chain hinge targeting hamstrings and glutes.
3. **Dumbbell Bent-Over Row** (`3 × 10`) — Bilateral upper back and lat development.
4. **Kettlebell Glute Bridge** (`3 × 12`) — Targeted glute activation and hip extension.
5. **Standing Dumbbell Overhead Press** (`2 × 10`) — Vertical pressing and shoulder stability.
6. **Plank** (`3 × 20–30 sec`) — Isometric anterior core anti-extension hold.

---

### Day 3: Full Body Strength B (Upper Emphasis)
Upper-body led session emphasizing horizontal push/pull, unilateral leg balance, and rotational stability.

1. **Dumbbell Chest Press** (`3 × 10`) — Horizontal pressing for chest, front shoulders, and triceps (floor or bench).
2. **Kettlebell Deadlift** (`3 × 10`) — Ground-based hip hinge building foundational hip power.
3. **Dumbbell Single-Arm Row** (`3 × 10 / side`) — Supported unilateral rowing to correct muscular imbalances.
4. **Dumbbell Reverse Lunge** (`3 × 8 / side`) — Unilateral leg strength and hip knee-tracking control.
5. **Kettlebell Halo** (`2 × 8 / side`) — Shoulder mobility, rotator cuff health, and core bracing.
6. **Dead Bug** (`3 × 10 / side`) — Controlled anti-extension and cross-body core coordination.

---

### Day 5: Full Body Strength C (Power & Core)
Combines dual-dumbbell loading, explosive hip drive, and multi-angle core stability.

1. **Dumbbell Front Squat** (`3 × 10`) — Quad loading with shoulder-racked dumbbells demanding thoracic extension.
2. **Dumbbell Renegade Row** (`3 × 8 / side`) — High-plank row developing lat strength and anti-rotational core stability without lower back fatigue.
3. **Kettlebell Swing** (`3 × 12`) — Explosive posterior chain power driven by hip snap.
4. **Dumbbell Lateral Raise** (`2 × 12`) — Shoulder isolation for lateral deltoid development.
5. **Bird Dog** (`3 × 8 / side`) — Posterior core stability and hip-shoulder cross-patterning.
6. **Side Plank** (`2 × 15–20 sec / side`) — Lateral core and oblique stability.

---

## 6-Week Progression Guide

- **Weeks 1–2 (Acclimatization & Form)**:
  - Choose weights that feel moderate (leaving 3–4 reps in reserve).
  - Focus on tempo, full depth, and feeling the target muscles contract.
- **Weeks 3–4 (Building Consistency)**:
  - Add 1–2 reps per set or slightly increase weight if technique is clean.
  - Increase plank hold times by 5–10 seconds.
- **Weeks 5–6 (Consolidation)**:
  - Solidify confidence with compound movements.
  - Ensure movements feel automatic, controlled, and stable.

---

## Training Rules & Safety

- **Listen to Joints**: Muscle burn is normal; joint pain is a signal to stop, reduce load, or modify the movement.
- **Rest & Breathing**: Exhale on exertion (pushing or standing up), inhale on lowering. Rest 60–90 seconds between sets.
- **Consult a Professional**: If you have pre-existing injuries or medical conditions, consult a physician or physiotherapist before starting.
